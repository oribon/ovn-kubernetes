EgressIP today

A stable source IP for external traffic from selected pods.

EgressIP selects pods, not destinations or ports.

Destination and port selection

TrafficClass matches a destination CIDR and optional protocol and ports. Multiple EgressIPs can serve the same pod.

Selective ASelective BUnmatched traffic uses the catch-all, or normal egress.

Two objects, linked by labels

TrafficClass defines the match. EgressIP supplies the source address.

TrafficClass

kind: TrafficClass
metadata:
  labels:
    demo.ovn.org/backend: a
spec:
  matches:
    - destinationCIDR: 10.89.0.8/32
      protocols:
        - tcp:
            destinationPort:
              number: 8080

EgressIP

kind: EgressIP
spec:
  egressIPs: [10.89.0.253]
  podSelector:
    matchLabels:
      app: egress-ip-demo-client
  trafficSelector:
    matchLabels:
      demo.ovn.org/backend: a

TCP, UDP, and SCTP. Source and destination ports accept a single number or an inclusive range. Omit protocols to match all traffic to the CIDR.
